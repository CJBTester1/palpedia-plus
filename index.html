What this guide considers “current”

Target: Palworld PS5 v1.0.5. The v1.0.5 patch was a small bug-fix update. The structured 1.0 Pal tables used here remain applicable because the current table-diff source reports no v1.0.5 changes to Pal stats, drops, Partner Skills, items or recipes.

Roster accounting: the game/marketing count and data-file count differ. The app loads every record exposed by the current structured dataset, including variants/collaboration forms. Astralym is separately marked by source data as a Paldeck-only story boss and not obtainable.

Coordinates: Alpha strings use exact coordinates when supplied. Regular habitat coordinates are approximate region-marker centres, not invented spawn points or region boundaries.

Build calculator: Potential is modelled separately for HP, Attack and Defense (0–100 talent = 0–30% level-scaling bonus). Condensation applies +5% HP/Attack/Defense per star, Souls provide 20 × 3% enhancement levels per stat, passive stat bonuses stack additively within their bonus category, and ordinary Alpha status applies the documented 1.2× HP scaling. Known legendary Alpha HP scaling exceptions are handled separately. Awakening is modelled as separate additive HP/Attack/Defense components from current v1.0 game-verified extraction evidence, rather than as a blanket percentage. Trust uses per-species Friendship_Stat values loaded from the current palworld-save-pal game-data extraction and is added before level/Potential scaling and later permanent multipliers.

Skill recommendations: “recommended” skills are an explainable efficiency heuristic, not an official tier list. Natural skills are ranked mainly by power/cooldown with same-element skills used as a tie-breaker; where community-measured boss DPS exists, the measurement is displayed separately.
